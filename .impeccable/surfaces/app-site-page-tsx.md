# Surface brief: storefront home (and the storefront routes that inherit it)

Scope: public storefront, home first; collections, category, product and visit pages inherit. Mode: Persuade (entry story is Experience-flavoured but must end in the working action). Admin is a separate Operate surface that inherits palette and type only.

Audience/job: Bengali families on phones choosing a specific gold piece (bridal forms first) and sending it to the shop on WhatsApp. Proof: real catalogue photos, weights, today's rate, BIS HUID. Constraints: no invented history, prices only as labelled estimates, skippable entry story, reduced-motion fallback, mid-range Android performance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every page is a hand-lettered Calcutta book jacket where the ornament's own Bengali name is the illustration. Refuses the black-and-ivory serif carousel every jeweller ships.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched flat letterpress fields (haldi #E2A426, sindoor #A3122A, neel #1C2766, morpankhi #0E6B66) with brush-black ink #16100D and kagoj paper #EFE2C4 kept to reading panels. Two-ink misregistration on lettering and line art, paper grain. Galada brush lettering for giant Bengali words; Anek Bangla/Latin with animated width axis for titles and UI; Martian Mono only for the stamped hallmark data line. Product photos matted as plates on fields, never tinted. Haldi-gold rationed to what acts on dark fields. Raises: hallmark data line (passport), colour rationing (Rietveld), stepping rate digits (depot blind), gram ruler (oscilloscope), named story cues (cyclorama), total field commitment (indoor sun).

STORY: Entry cues Mati, Agun, Haturi, Alta show gold becoming a bride's ornament; the visitor lands on the jacket, sees today's rate and the bridal forms by name, picks a category spine, opens a piece, reads its honest numbers, and taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Haldi jacket. Sindoor spine on the left (logo, menu). সোনা in Galada across ~70% width, a two-ink brush drawing of a wrist in sankha, pola and noa threaded through the letters. Roman title bottom-left at display scale. Round navy rate sticker top-right with stepping digits. Bottom flap: "Find your piece" row of lettered category chips plus WhatsApp action, visible without scrolling.
